:::

::: {#ask-personal-question .choice-row data-lookup="你鸡巴多少厘米？"}

### 累不累？ {#kumade-check-in}

::: {.choice-list data-place="下午"}
::: {#tell-kumade-tired .choice-row data-lookup="很累"}
[累]{.lookup-name} [+4 GP]{.gp-in}

:::
:::
